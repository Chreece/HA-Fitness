# Fitness for Home Assistant

**Make your fitness data work together — live, after the workout, and over time.**

Fitness is a person-centered Home Assistant integration that merges compatible fitness, workout and sleep data into one local model. It can combine live sensors, completed workouts, recovery data and your own history, then turn them into useful dashboards, comparisons, automations and optional coaching.

</div>

## TL;DR

Fitness combines compatible fitness data already available in Home Assistant into one person-centered model. Your wearable integration remains the source for provider data; Fitness adds live sensor merging, completed-workout reconciliation, recovery, personal baselines, RPE, optional coaching, cards and automations.

**Deterministic calculations are the product core. AI is only an interpretation layer.** Scientific metrics, formulas, workout merging, readiness components, RPE load and other deterministic results are **never generated by AI**. AI can turn already-calculated evidence into natural-language coaching or summaries when you enable it.

You can use any compatible Home Assistant source. For example, **HA-ANT-Plus** can supply live heart rate, speed, cadence or power while a watch integration later contributes the completed workout.

## Why Fitness?

Your watch is excellent at being a watch. Home Assistant is excellent at connecting things.

Fitness sits between them: it can use data from multiple integrations and sensors instead of forcing one vendor to be the whole story. A heart-rate strap can contribute live HR, another sensor can provide speed or power, a watch integration can later provide the completed workout, and Fitness can merge those views into one workout rather than treating them as unrelated sessions.

That makes some fun things possible — lights that follow workout intensity, spoken progress updates, a dashboard on the wall — but the serious part is underneath: normalized units, source provenance, personal baselines, validated history, transparent formulas and evidence-backed metrics.

## What can it do?

- **Live workouts** — combine available live HR, speed, cadence, power, distance and other compatible sensors.
- **Completed workouts** — normalize and merge workouts from supported Home Assistant integrations.
- **Recovery** — combine sleep, HRV, resting HR, recent training and post-exercise recovery into personal context.
- **RPE** — record how hard a workout felt, import it from providers when available, and calculate session-RPE load.
- **Personal comparisons** — compare like with like against your own validated Fitness history.
- **Strength analysis** — optional detailed sets/reps/volume, estimated 1RM and exercise progression.
- **Coaching** — optional localized TTS/AI announcements and Home Assistant light feedback.
- **Dashboards & automations** — Fitness entities are normal Home Assistant entities and can control anything Home Assistant can control.

Fitness does **not** invent missing measurements. Features appear only when the required evidence exists.

## Why not just use my Garmin / Polar / watch integration?

You should keep it — Fitness is designed to **use** those integrations, not replace them.

A provider integration normally tells Home Assistant what that provider knows. Fitness adds the cross-provider layer: it can merge the watch's completed workout with live sensors, retain Fitness-owned HR recovery or RPE, compare the result with canonical personal history, and expose one consistent set of entities regardless of where each measurement originated.

See **[FAQ](docs/FAQ.md)** for examples and the longer answer.

## Is this scientifically serious?

Yes, with an important boundary: Fitness is a **training and wellness tool, not a medical device**.

Where a calculation has an established method, Fitness keeps the formula, data used and scientific basis auditable. Personal baselines are preferred where universal cutoffs would be misleading. Fitness-owned composite interpretations are explicitly identified as such instead of being presented as clinical measurements.

Read **[Science & methods](docs/SCIENCE.md)** for the evidence and design rules.

## Getting started

1. Install Fitness through HACS as a custom repository, or copy `custom_components/fitness/` into your Home Assistant configuration.
2. Restart Home Assistant.
3. Go to **Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → Fitness**.
4. Create a profile and select the Home Assistant devices that contain your live, workout and recovery data.
5. Optionally configure coaching, speakers, notifications and lights.
6. Add the Fitness cards/dashboard and train.

The integration creates separate logical devices for **Live workout**, **Workouts**, **Recovery**, and **Evaluation** so raw measurements, completed sessions and interpretation stay understandable.

## Fitness dashboard

Fitness includes Home Assistant **Community dashboards/cards** for live workouts, completed workouts, recovery and evaluation. They use normalized Fitness entities, so the UI does not need to know whether a value originally came from a watch, Sleep as Android, ANT+, Bluetooth or another supported source.

Normally the frontend resource is registered automatically. If a manual resource is ever needed for troubleshooting, the module path is `/fitness/frontend/fitness-dashboard.js`.

Workout routes use **OpenStreetMap** tiles when the current merged workout has valid GPS data. No map is shown when the workout has no matching route.

For card behavior, merging rules and available views, see **[Features & data flow](docs/FEATURES.md)**.

## Privacy and philosophy

Fitness reads the Home Assistant entities you select. It does not need to replace your wearable integration or log directly into every fitness service. Deterministic calculations work without AI; AI is an optional interpretation/coaching layer.

The goal is simple: **keep your data useful, explain where results came from, and let your smart home participate in your training without pretending that a dashboard is a doctor.**
